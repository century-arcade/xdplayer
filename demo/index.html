xdplayer

A crossword player for any web page: one script, no dependencies. It plays .xd, the plain-text crossword format. Click a square or a clue and type. Arrows move, Shift-arrows strafe, Tab goes to the next clue, Space turns, Escape enters a rebus.

<script src="xd-player.js"></script>
<xd-player src="puzzle.xd"></xd-player>
